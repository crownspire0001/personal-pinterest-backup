Privacy Policy

Personal Backup is a personal-use application operated by Overload.

Purpose

This application is used solely by its owner to back up and manage the owner's own Pinterest boards, Pins, and associated metadata.

Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner, the application may access Pinterest account information, boards, Pins, and related metadata necessary to perform the requested backup.

Data Storage

Downloaded Pinterest data is stored locally on the owner's computer. The application does not operate a remote database or sell, rent, or distribute the downloaded information.

Credentials

The application does not request or store the owner's Pinterest password. Pinterest authentication is performed through Pinterest's authorized authentication system.

Third Parties

The application does not sell or share the owner's Pinterest data with third parties.

Contact

For questions regarding this application, contact the application owner through the associated GitHub account.
